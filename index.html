Welcome

Shreyaah's Bliss Trails is a vibrant space in Kolkata that offers a wide range of life-enrichment programmes, workshops, and cultural events. This studio brings together traditional craftsmanship, considered design and eastern–western wellness traditions.

Our work is dedicated to creative expression, conscious living and community. We offer ways to beautify your everyday life and support your journey towards growth and self-discovery.
