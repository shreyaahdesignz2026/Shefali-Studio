Welcome

Shreyaah's Bliss Trails is a vibrant space in Kolkata that offers a wide range of gifting solutions, self-development programmes, workshops, and cultural events. Co-created by a dynamic mother–daughter duo, this studio brings together traditional craftsmanship, considered design, and eastern–western wellness traditions.

Our work is dedicated to creative expression, conscious living and community. Allow us to beautify your everyday life and support your journey towards growth and self-discovery.

What Makes This Different

One source, many mindful offerings

Craft and inner work under one roof

Most studios do one or the other. Here, the handmade products and the holistic sessions come from the same place and the same thinking — which is why a journal, a candle and a consultation can belong to the same conversation.

Made by a mother–daughter studio

The studio is co-created by two people, not assembled by a committee. What gets made, and what gets offered, reflects that directly.

Handcrafted in small batches

Candles are hand-poured in small batches; the terracotta, jute and handcrafted collections are made with local artisans and crafters across Bengal, whose heritage skills shape the work.

Sessions led by trained facilitators

Group classes and workshops are guided by certified practitioners, therapists and experts in their fields — chosen for expertise and for how they hold a room.
