Your Data

What we would collect, why, and who can see it. Written plainly, because you should not have to read a policy to know this.

What is collected
Your name, email, phone number, and up to five delivery addresses. Your orders, bookings, wishlist and any profile note you write.
Why
To confirm and deliver your orders, to hold and remind you about your bookings, and to reach you when something you asked about changes.
What other members can see
Only your display name, your short profile note and your listed interests — and only inside the Members Lounge and Tribe.
What stays private
Your email, phone number, addresses, payment details, orders and bookings. These are never visible to other members.
What you can edit
All of your personal details, your addresses, your profile and your wishlist, at any time.
If you close your account
Your profile and wishlist are removed. Order and booking records are kept only as long as tax and accounting rules require.
Needs legal review
This plain-language summary must be checked against the full Privacy Policy and reviewed by a qualified professional before accounts open, particularly the retention periods.
