Pulp & Paper

Every notebook we create begins with intention. From concept to cover, each design is envisioned by our in-house team to inspire creativity, clarity and elegance. We use ethically sourced, premium recycled pages — some featuring plantable seed paper covers designed to bloom after use, others wrapped in hand-finished cloth.

Our handmade paper notebooks bring traditional artistry into modern hands, working with heritage craft forms integrated into contemporary designs — luxury that uplifts communities, not just lifestyles.

Explore a Balanced Holistic Lifestyle

Balance is not about perfection — it's about presence. Mindful mornings, nourishing food, joyful movement, digital detoxes and gratitude. Holistic living begins with small, meaningful steps: listening to your body, exploring mind–body practices, mindful eating, seeking community, and continuing to learn.

It extends to mindful consumption too — supporting artisans, choosing sustainable materials, buying less but better, and engaging with brands that care.

Embracing Equilibrium: A Journey Towards Harmony

History has long enforced the dominance of one polarity over another — patriarchy over matriarchy, solar over lunar, masculine over feminine. What's needed now isn't the reversal of that imbalance but equilibrium: power with, not power over.

A reflection born from a Family Constellation session with Geetu Thackur, on connection, mindfulness, and the ripple effect of inner balance on the world around us.

Beginning of Blissful Trails

A space where craft meets consciousness. Rebranded in 2025 from Bowl of Bliss, Blissful Trails offers oracle card readings, chakra guidance, spirit and power animal messages, and grounding breathwork — rooted in Shreyaah's own journey through Angel Therapy, Reiki, Lama Fera, Access Bars and shamanic study.

Not a healer's title, but a space held with humility, humour and heart.
