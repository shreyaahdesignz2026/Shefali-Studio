The Artisoul Tribe

For those who return not simply to shop, but to belong.

Create · Connect · Heal · Grow

What it is

The Artisoul Tribe is the studio's membership community, held for kindred spirits who resonate with the values behind the work — mindful living, handcrafted intention, and a slower, more soulful way of being.

Draft concept — not yet launched
No membership programme currently exists. Everything below is a first-draft concept written in the studio's voice, based on member-only features referenced elsewhere in the sitemap (Bulletin, Members Lounge, event pre-access). Please treat every specific benefit as a suggestion to confirm or replace.

Suggested Benefits

What belonging could mean

Not “exclusive access” for its own sake — practical things that make it easier to take part in what the studio actually does.

Early access to new work

Gracious early access to new product releases and Limited Collection drops, before they open more widely.

Priority on events

Priority or discounted registration for events, workshops and gatherings such as Alaap.

The members-only Bulletin

Studio announcements, previews and behind-the-scenes updates on the Notice Board, visible only to members.

A profile, and a place to talk

A visible member profile and a seat in the Members Lounge discussion forum.

Member-only offers

Occasional offers surfaced gently through the Special Offer feature.

Smaller gatherings

Invitations to more intimate gatherings, held apart from the wider public events.
