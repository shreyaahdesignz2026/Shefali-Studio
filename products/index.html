On sustainability claims

Where the studio describes a piece as eco-friendly, it is because of something specific — terracotta and natural materials, small-batch making, or work with local artisans. We have deliberately not applied a blanket sustainability badge across the catalogue, because a claim that cannot be explained is not worth making.

Materials, packaging and care information for individual pieces are still being documented and will be added per product.
