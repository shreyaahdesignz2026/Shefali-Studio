At the Centre of the Work

The Blissful Trails Process

This is the studio's own framework, not an established external modality. It is called a process rather than a session because it unfolds across six to nine meetings — long enough for something to actually shift, rather than be described once and left there.

It draws on the practices the studio works with: oracle card readings, chakra guidance, spirit and power animal messages, and gentle breathwork and grounding rituals. What gets used, and in what order, depends on the person in front of us.

This section needs Shreyaah's own words

Still to be written: what happens across the sessions, what "personalised" means here, the role of consultation and reflection, what a participant actually experiences, and the intended direction of the process. What is not promised

No diagnosis, no cure, no guaranteed outcome, and no prediction of the future. This is guidance-oriented work intended to support self-reflection and general well-being. It does not replace medical, psychological, legal or financial advice, and it is not a substitute for treatment you may already be receiving.

The Small Print

Cancellation policy

This policy applies to all Oracle Card Reading sessions conducted under Designz Life / Blissful Trails, in both online and in-person formats, and to all individual sessions.

All sessions are strictly by prior appointment only. Bookings are confirmed solely upon receipt of 100% advance payment, inclusive of applicable Indian taxes. Confirmation of the appointment date and time will be provided by the team once payment is credited to our account.

Full cancellations are not permitted. Clients may request one reschedule only, and such a request must be made at least 72 hours prior to the scheduled session date — for example, for a Monday appointment the request must reach us by Friday night.

Rescheduled sessions will be offered within 15 days to 1 month from the original booking date, subject to availability.

Any request made within 48 hours of the session, or after one reschedule has already been used, will not be accommodated and will be treated as a no-show.

Sessions are designed for one-to-one consultation only. If an additional participant wishes to attend, prior written intimation is required. Acceptance and any applicable additional charges will be discussed separately.

If the client does not attend the confirmed session at the appointed time, online or in person, it shall be treated as a no-show. No-shows are non-refundable and non-adjustable.

In the rare event that Designz Life, Blissful Trails, or Ms. Shreya Saha must cancel or reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances or emergencies, clients will be offered either a full refund of the amount paid, or a fresh appointment date within one month of the original confirmed booking.

Oracle Card Reading is a guidance-oriented service intended to support self-reflection, inner growth and holistic well-being. It does not replace medical, legal, financial or professional advice. Clients are responsible for their own decisions and actions following the session.

Any disputes shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Kolkata, West Bengal.
