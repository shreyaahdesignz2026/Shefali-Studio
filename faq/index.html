Brand

A vibrant space in Kolkata offering gifting solutions, self-development programmes, workshops and cultural events. It is co-created by a mother–daughter duo, and it brings together traditional craftsmanship, considered design and eastern–western wellness traditions.

In practice, two things happen in the same place: objects made by hand, and sessions held with care.

Read more about the studio.

Designz Life is the handcrafted design and gifting practice the studio grew out of, begun in 2013 and registered as an MSME studio in 2024. The name still appears on policies, invoices and social channels. Shreyaah's Bliss Trails is the name of the studio as you meet it here.

Blissful Trails is the wellness side — oracle readings, chakra guidance, energy work and reflective practice, mostly one-to-one. It was rebranded in 2025 from Bowl of Bliss.

The Enrichment Clubhouse is the learning and gathering side — yoga, sound healing, art therapy, mindful parenting, journalling circles and book club, mostly in groups.

Booking

Choose the path that suits you on the Services page — Personal Journey, Collective Circle, or Custom Collaboration — and follow the enquiry form. You can also simply message us on WhatsApp.

All sessions are strictly by prior appointment. Your booking is confirmed once 100% advance payment is received, inclusive of applicable Indian taxes, and the team confirms your date and time.

Bookings are confirmed on full advance payment. Full cancellations are not permitted. Should your plans change, one reschedule is accommodated, provided it is requested at least 72 hours before your session.

Requests made within 48 hours, or a second reschedule, are treated as a no-show and remain non-refundable. Rescheduled sessions are offered within 15 days to 1 month of the original booking, subject to availability.

Read the complete policy.

If we must cancel or reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances, you are offered either a full refund of the amount paid, or a fresh appointment within one month of the original confirmed booking. Your choice.
