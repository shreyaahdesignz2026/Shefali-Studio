Booking policy

Published in full This is the one policy currently published in detail. It applies to all Oracle Card Reading sessions conducted under Designz Life / Blissful Trails, in both online and in-person formats.

This policy applies to all Oracle Card Reading sessions conducted under Designz Life / Blissful Trails in both online and in-person formats.

All sessions are strictly by prior appointment only. Bookings are confirmed solely upon receipt of 100% advance payment, inclusive of applicable Indian taxes. Confirmation of the appointment date and time will be provided by the team once payment is credited to our account.

Sessions are designed for one-to-one consultation only. If an additional participant wishes to attend, prior written intimation is required. Acceptance and applicable additional charges will be discussed separately.

Any disputes shall fall under the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Kolkata, West Bengal.

Refund & cancellation

Full cancellations are not permitted. Clients may request one reschedule only, and such request must be made at least 72 hours prior to the scheduled session date — for example, for a Monday appointment the request must reach us by Friday night.

Rescheduled sessions will be offered within 15 days to 1 month from the original booking date, subject to availability. Any request made within 48 hours of the session, or after one reschedule has already been utilised, will not be accommodated and will be treated as a no-show.

If the client does not attend the confirmed session at the appointed time, online or in person, it shall be treated as a no-show. No-shows are non-refundable and non-adjustable.

In the rare event that Designz Life, Blissful Trails, or Ms. Shreya Saha must cancel or reschedule due to unforeseen circumstances or emergencies, clients will be offered either a full refund of the amount paid, or a fresh appointment date within one month of the original confirmed booking.
